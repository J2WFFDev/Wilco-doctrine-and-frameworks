# Volunteer Guide

---

## Welcome

Wilco Shooting Sports depends on volunteers to operate. Your time and effort directly support the athletes in this program. Thank you.

This guide explains your roles, what we need from you, and the safety standards all volunteers must follow.

---

## Volunteer Roles

| Role | Primary Responsibilities |
|------|--------------------------|
| **Range Setup** | Stage construction, target placement, equipment staging |
| **Range Officer (RO)** | Scoring, range commands, safety monitoring during competition |
| **Scorekeeper** | Logging scores, times, and results during match |
| **Equipment Handler** | Resetting targets, moving props, maintaining stage readiness |
| **Registration / Admin** | Check-in, paperwork, match administration support |
| **Safety Monitor** | Observing range activity for safety violations, supporting RO |

You will be assigned a role before each event. If your assignment is unclear, ask the match coordinator before the event begins.

---

## Range Safety — All Volunteers

Safety is the responsibility of every person on the range — not just the Range Officers.

**If you observe an unsafe act:**
- Call "CEASE FIRE" loudly and clearly
- Do not hesitate — safety takes precedence over everything
- Notify the Range Officer and coach immediately

**All volunteers must:**
- Know the four fundamental safety rules (see [operations/safety-manual.md](../operations/safety-manual.md))
- Maintain awareness of muzzle direction at all times
- Keep spectators behind the safety line
- Never handle a firearm unless specifically assigned to do so and trained for it

---

## Range Officer Responsibilities

If you are serving as a Range Officer:

1. Brief each athlete on the course of fire before they shoot
2. Give range commands clearly and at the appropriate time
3. Call "STOP" for any safety concern without hesitation
4. Score accurately and transparently; explain calls when asked
5. Report any safety incidents to the match coordinator immediately

> See [operations/match-operations.md](../operations/match-operations.md) for full RO procedures and range command scripts.

---

## Setup and Teardown

- Arrive at the assigned time for setup — not at the match start time
- Follow the setup diagram or coordinator instructions for stage configuration
- Check that all targets are properly placed and secure before the match begins
- Assist with teardown until the range is fully cleared and equipment is stored

---

## Communication

- Direct questions about the match to the match coordinator
- Direct questions about athlete performance or training to the coach — not the athlete or parent
- If you are unsure about a call or procedure, ask before deciding unilaterally

---

## Conduct Expectations

Volunteers represent Wilco Shooting Sports to competitors, parents, and the public.

- Be professional, patient, and consistent
- Treat all athletes — regardless of age or skill level — with equal respect
- Do not coach athletes during competition; that is the coach's role
- Model the sportsmanship we expect from athletes and families
