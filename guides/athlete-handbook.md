# Athlete Handbook

> **Canonical terminology reference:** [doctrine/terminology.md](../doctrine/terminology.md)

---

## Welcome

This handbook is your guide to competing and developing as an athlete in Wilco Shooting Sports. Read it, keep it, and refer to it. It covers what is expected of you, how training works, and how to prepare for competition.

---

## Athlete Expectations

- Arrive on time and prepared for every session.
- Bring all required equipment in safe, functioning condition.
- Follow all range safety rules without exception. See [operations/safety-manual.md](../operations/safety-manual.md).
- Give full effort in every drill and every string.
- Be coachable: receive feedback without defensiveness, apply it with intention.
- Conduct yourself with respect toward coaches, teammates, competitors, and range staff at all times.

---

## Training Routine

### Before Each Session
- Review your current OKR goals (see [terminology.md — OKR](../doctrine/terminology.md#okr))
- Perform a physical warm-up
- Dry-fire practice if time and location allow
- Set your intention for the session: what specifically are you working on?

### During Each Session
- Execute drills with full focus — quality over speed
- Use the [PARR cycle](../doctrine/terminology.md#parr) for every string: Prepare → Act → Review → Repeat
- Call your shots and log your results
- Ask questions; seek feedback actively

### After Each Session
- Log your session data (bio-condition, environment, results)
- Identify the single most important thing you learned
- Note what to carry into the next session

---

## Competition Preparation

### Stage Preparation
1. Walk every stage before shooting
2. Identify target order and plan your transitions
3. Plan your foot position and start position
4. Mentally rehearse the full string before approaching the line

### String Preparation
1. Establish stance and grip before the beep
2. Run through your pre-shot routine
3. Commit to your plan — shoot what you prepared

### Post-Match Review
- Use the PARR framework to assess each stage
- Classify each run (clean, sloppy, recovery, miss)
- Identify the one highest-leverage improvement area
- Bring that focus into the next training session

---

## Equipment Responsibilities

- Know your equipment. Understand how your firearm operates.
- Maintain your equipment in safe, reliable condition.
- Report any equipment malfunction to a coach before continuing to shoot.
- Do not modify equipment without discussing with your coach first.

---

## Program Culture

You represent Wilco Shooting Sports on every range, not just at team events. That means:

- Acknowledge good shooting by competitors, win or lose.
- Accept your results without excuse or blame.
- Support your teammates, especially when they are struggling.
- Behave as someone the program is proud to have as a member.
