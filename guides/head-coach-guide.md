# Head Coach Guide

> **Canonical terminology reference:** [doctrine/terminology.md](../doctrine/terminology.md)
>
> **Role distinction:** The Head Coach provides program-level leadership and development oversight. The [Coach Guide](coach-guide.md) covers the day-to-day athlete coaching role. Where they overlap, this guide takes precedence for the Head Coach's responsibilities.

---

## Role of the Head Coach

The Head Coach is responsible for the **development environment** — not just individual athlete performance. This means the Head Coach is accountable for:

- Coach development and standards
- Practice structure and quality
- Athlete development tracking across the full roster
- Program culture
- Communication with the Program Director
- Alignment between coaching behavior and program doctrine

## Head Coach vs. Other Coaching Roles

| Role | Primary Focus | Scope |
|------|--------------|-------|
| **Head Coach** | Program leadership and coach development | Full program |
| **Practice Coach** | Athlete development at the range | Session-level |
| **Match Coach** | Athlete support and strategy during competition | Match-day |
