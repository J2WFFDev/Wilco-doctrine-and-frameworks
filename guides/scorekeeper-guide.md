## Role of the Scorekeeper

The Scorekeeper captures accurate, complete match data for every competitor on their assigned stage or squad. Scorekeeper accuracy directly affects competitor results, awards, and the program's data records.

The Scorekeeper is responsible for:

- Recording times and misses accurately for each string
- Confirming data with the competitor after each stage
- Coordinating with timer operators and tablets
- Escalating disputes to the Match Director — not resolving them independently
