## 1. Draw to First Shot

**Target Component:** Presentation

**Setup:**
- One steel target at 10 yards
- Shooter at low-ready or in holster

**Procedure:**
1. On signal, draw (or raise from low-ready) and fire one shot at the target
2. Reset; repeat × 5
3. Log each result (hit/miss, felt quality of draw)

**Metric:** Time from signal to hit; consistency across 5 repetitions

**Standard:**

| Stage | Target Time |
|-------|-------------|
| Foundation | Consistent hits; time secondary |
| Development | < 1.5s draw-to-hit |
| Performance | < 1.0s draw-to-hit |

**Notes:**
- Prioritize a consistent [presentation](../doctrine/terminology.md#presentation) over speed
- Common error: rushing the draw, losing grip
- Coaching cue: "See the sights before you press"

## 2. Controlled Pairs

**Target Component:** Target Engagement

**Setup:**
- One steel target at 7–10 yards
- Shooter at low-ready

**Procedure:**
1. On signal, engage the target with two shots (double-tap)
2. [Call both shots](../doctrine/terminology.md#shot-calling) before checking
3. Reset; repeat × 5

**Metric:** Split time between shots; call accuracy (called vs. actual)

**Standard:**

| Stage | Target Split |
|-------|-------------|
| Foundation | < 0.60s |
| Development | < 0.45s |
| Performance | < 0.30s |

**Notes:**
- Emphasize [trigger reset](../doctrine/terminology.md#trigger-reset) between shots
- Athletes must call both shots before moving to reset
- Sloppy splits are better than rushed misses

## 3. Two-Target Transition

**Target Component:** Transition Efficiency

**Setup:**
- Two steel targets, 6 feet apart, at 10 yards
- Shooter at low-ready

**Procedure:**
1. On signal, engage Target A, then Target B (one shot each)
2. Log split time (A shot to B shot)
3. Repeat in reverse (B → A)
4. × 5 each direction

**Metric:** Transition split time; hit rate on second target

**Standard:**

| Stage | Target Transition Split |
|-------|------------------------|
| Foundation | < 0.70s; consistent hits |
| Development | < 0.55s |
| Performance | < 0.40s |

**Notes:**
- Eyes should lead the muzzle in transitions
- Common error: rushing transition, losing sight picture on arrival
- Coaching cue: "Land and confirm before you press"

## 4. Stop Plate Isolation

**Target Component:** Target Engagement (Stop Plate)

**Setup:**
- One 8" stop plate at 18–25 yards
- Shooter at low-ready

**Procedure:**
1. On signal, engage stop plate; one shot
2. Log result (hit/miss)
3. × 10 repetitions

**Metric:** Hit rate across 10 shots (goal: 9/10 minimum)

**Standard:**

| Stage | Hit Rate |
|-------|---------|
| Development | 7/10 |
| Performance | 9/10 |

**Notes:**
- Stop plate misses cost significant time in matches
- Use [confirmed execution](../doctrine/terminology.md#confirmed-execution) — never predictive — on stop plate
- Coaching cue: "Patience on the stop plate is free time"

## 5. Full Stage Simulation

**Target Component:** All

**Setup:**
- Full Steel Challenge stage layout (e.g., Five to Go, Showdown)
- Standard match conditions

**Procedure:**
1. Complete a full stage: 5 strings, stop plate each string
2. Classify each string (clean, sloppy, recovery, miss)
3. Conduct PARR review after each string
4. Log stage time, per-string times, and classification

**Metric:** Stage time; run classification distribution; stop plate hit rate

**Notes:**
- Use this drill to simulate match pressure
- The goal is process quality, not just total time
- Debrief: "What was the dominant run type today? What drove it?"
