# Canonical Terminology

> **Rule:** This is the single source of truth for all program terminology. Other documents link here — they do not redefine these terms. If a term is missing or incorrect, update it here.

---

## Performance & Execution Terms

### Impact Assessment
The real-time evaluation of where a shot landed relative to the intended point of impact. Used to determine whether the shooter's call matched the outcome and whether a correction is needed.

### Shot Calling
The shooter's declaration of where they believe the shot landed at the moment of the shot break — before looking at the target. Accurate shot calling indicates strong visual focus and sight picture maintenance through the shot.

### Trigger Prep
The application of controlled, progressive pressure to the trigger prior to the final firing sequence. Trigger prep eliminates slack and stages the trigger for a clean break without disturbing sight alignment.

### Trigger Reset
The intentional forward release of the trigger to the reset point following a shot, in preparation for the next shot. Proper reset ensures consistency and prevents trigger slap.

### Shot Break
The moment the firearm discharges. Specifically, the quality of the shot break — whether it was clean (no movement) or disturbed (flinch, anticipation, push).

### Predictive Execution
A firing mode in which the shooter fires based on an anticipated sight picture, typically used in Steel Challenge when the shot is fired as the muzzle arrives on target. High-risk, high-reward; requires a well-developed presentation and strong internalized target geometry.

### Confirmed Execution
A firing mode in which the shooter fires only after visually confirming an acceptable sight picture on the target. Safer, more accurate, but slower than predictive execution.

### Transition Efficiency
The speed and accuracy with which a shooter moves from one target to the next. Measured by the time from the shot break on one target to the first shot break on the next, relative to the distance and difficulty of the transition.

### Presentation
The complete movement from holster (or low-ready) to the point where the firearm is on target and the shooter is ready to fire. Includes draw, grip, stance, and sight alignment establishment.

## Run Classification Terms

### Clean Run
A stage run in which all targets were engaged successfully, no procedural errors occurred, and execution was consistent with the shooter's trained process. May not be the fastest run.

### Sloppy Run
A stage run in which hits were achieved but execution deviated from the trained process — through anticipation, poor transitions, or inconsistent trigger work. May be fast but is not a reliable model to repeat.

### Recovery Run
A stage run in which one or more errors occurred (miss, procedural fault, equipment issue) and the shooter successfully recognized and corrected the error during the string. Quality of recovery is evaluated, not the raw time.

## Stage & Match Terms

### Stage Preparation
The process of analyzing a stage before shooting — walking the stage, identifying target order, planning transitions, selecting foot positions, and rehearsing the plan mentally or physically.

### String Preparation
The immediate pre-firing routine performed before each string within a stage. Includes establishing stance, grip, mental readiness, and calling the shot plan.

### PARR
**Prepare · Act · Review · Repeat**
The four-phase performance cycle used at all levels of Wilco training. PARR structures both individual runs and longer development cycles.

| Phase | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Prepare** | Stage walk, mental rehearsal, string setup |
| **Act** | Execute the string or stage with full commitment |
| **Review** | Honest assessment of what happened vs. what was planned |
| **Repeat** | Apply the insight from the review into the next attempt |

### OKR
**Objectives and Key Results**
A goal-setting framework used to define what the athlete is working toward (Objective) and how progress will be measured (Key Results). OKRs are set at the session, match, and season level.

| Component | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Objective** | A qualitative, motivating goal statement |
| **Key Result** | A specific, measurable outcome that indicates progress |

### OGSM
**Objectives · Goals · Strategies · Measures**
A strategic planning framework used at the program level for seasonal planning and development milestones.

| Component | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Objective** | Broad directional statement |
| **Goal** | Specific, quantified target |
| **Strategy** | The approach taken to reach the goal |
| **Measure** | How progress and success are tracked |

## Equipment & Condition Terms

### Bio-Condition
The athlete's physical and mental state on a given training day or match day — including sleep, nutrition, stress, and physical readiness. Tracked as a factor when reviewing performance variance.
