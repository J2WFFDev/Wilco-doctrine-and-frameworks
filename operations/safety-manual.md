# Safety Manual

> **Safety supersedes all other program goals. No competitive objective, time pressure, or convenience justifies bypassing these rules.**

---

## The Four Fundamental Rules

These rules apply to every person on the range at all times — athletes, coaches, volunteers, and spectators.

1. **Treat every firearm as if it is loaded.** There is no such thing as an unloaded gun on the range.
2. **Never point the muzzle at anything you are not willing to destroy.** Muzzle awareness is a constant, active responsibility.
3. **Keep your finger off the trigger until your sights are on target and you have made the decision to shoot.** The trigger is not a resting position.
4. **Know your target and what is beyond it.** You are responsible for every round that leaves your firearm.

---

## Range Safety Rules

- **Eye and ear protection** are required on the firing line at all times.
- **Cold range:** Firearms are unloaded and holstered/cased unless explicitly authorized by a range officer.
- **Hot range:** Competitors may be loaded only during active competition, under RO supervision.
- **No handling of firearms** in staging areas or behind the firing line without RO authorization.
- **Alcohol and drugs:** No shooting or range presence while impaired. This is grounds for immediate permanent removal.
- **Spectators** must remain behind the designated spectator line at all times.
- **Children** who are not competing must be under direct adult supervision and behind the spectator line.

---

## Cease Fire Procedures

Anyone on the range may call a cease fire at any time for any safety concern.

**If you observe an unsafe condition:**
1. Call "CEASE FIRE" loudly and clearly
2. All shooting stops immediately
3. Notify the Range Officer or coach
4. Do not resume until the condition is resolved and the RO gives the all-clear

**Common reasons to call cease fire:**
- Muzzle pointed in an unsafe direction
- Finger on trigger when not shooting
- Downrange personnel during shooting
- Equipment malfunction with safety implications
- Competitor or bystander appears injured or in distress

## Emergency Procedures

### Medical Emergency
1. Call "CEASE FIRE" if shooting is in progress
2. Call 911 immediately
3. Notify Coach Jim West (program director)
4. Do not move an injured person unless they are in immediate danger
5. Keep the area clear for emergency responders
6. Document the incident fully

### Accidental Discharge
1. Call "CEASE FIRE"
2. Secure all firearms
3. Check for injuries
4. Do not disturb the scene until the incident is reviewed
5. Report to Coach Jim West immediately
6. Complete a written incident report before leaving the range

### Range Fire or Other Hazard
1. Call "CEASE FIRE"
2. Follow the facility's posted emergency evacuation plan
3. Account for all athletes, coaches, and volunteers
4. Notify facility staff and emergency services as needed

## Safety Violation Policy

| Violation Type | Response |
|----------------|---------|
| First muzzle violation | Immediate verbal correction; athlete removed from the line for coaching review |
| Second muzzle violation | Session suspension; formal review before next session |
| Third muzzle violation or any finger-in-trigger-guard violation | Program suspension pending review |
| Intentional or reckless violation | Immediate program removal |

Safety violations are documented. Documentation is maintained by Coach Jim West.

---

## Home Storage Requirements

All athletes and families are responsible for safe storage at home.

- Firearms must be stored in a locked container inaccessible to unauthorized users
- Ammunition must be stored separately from firearms
- Keys or combinations must not be accessible to minors
- State and local storage laws apply and must be followed
