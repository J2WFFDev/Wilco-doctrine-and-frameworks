## Daily Operations Checklist

### Opening (Before Athletes Arrive)
- [ ] Inspect range for hazards (debris, wet surfaces, equipment damage)
- [ ] Confirm eye and ear protection is available
- [ ] Set up targets per session plan
- [ ] Verify all timing equipment is functioning
- [ ] Establish spectator area and ensure it is clearly marked
- [ ] Confirm first aid kit is present and stocked

### Closing (After Session Ends)
- [ ] Confirm all firearms are unloaded and cased before teardown begins
- [ ] Remove and store all targets and props
- [ ] Collect all brass if required by facility
- [ ] Inspect range for left-behind equipment or hazards
- [ ] Log any equipment damage or maintenance needs
- [ ] Secure facility per facility requirements

---

## Stage Setup

### SASP Stage Setup
1. Reference the approved stage diagram for target positions
2. Measure and mark target positions before placing stands
3. Confirm target heights are within match specifications
4. Inspect all target stands for stability
5. Verify stop plate is clearly distinguishable from field targets
6. Walk the stage to confirm sight lines and shooter path are clear

### Target Inspection Standards
- Steel targets: inspect for damage, cracks, or unsafe pitting before each session
- Target stands: check for wobble or structural instability
- Remove any target that fails inspection; do not use damaged targets
