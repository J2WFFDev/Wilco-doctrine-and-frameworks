## Role of the Match Organizer

The Match Organizer is responsible for assembling registered competitors into squads before a hosted match. This role is distinct from the Match Director (who runs the match) and the Scorekeeper (who records results).

The Match Organizer:

- Collects competitor registrations and confirms eligibility
- Assembles squads based on defined criteria
- Publishes squad assignments to competitors and match staff
- Manages changes and late registrations
