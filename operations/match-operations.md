> **Canonical terminology reference:** [doctrine/terminology.md](../doctrine/terminology.md)

## Match Coordinator Checklist

### Pre-Match (Day Before)
- [ ] Confirm athlete registration and equipment compliance
- [ ] Assign RO and volunteer roles
- [ ] Confirm stage layouts and target availability
- [ ] Verify scoring system is ready
- [ ] Brief all volunteers on roles and safety expectations

### Match Day — Setup
- [ ] Arrive at range minimum 90 minutes before first shot
- [ ] Confirm stage setup matches approved layout
- [ ] Verify all targets are properly placed and functional
- [ ] Brief Range Officers on course of fire for each stage
- [ ] Conduct a safety brief for all competitors and spectators

### Match Day — During Competition
- [ ] Monitor stage flow and troubleshoot delays
- [ ] Ensure ROs are following protocol consistently
- [ ] Address equipment issues promptly
- [ ] Document any safety incidents immediately

### Post-Match — Teardown
- [ ] Confirm all firearms are unloaded and holstered/cased before teardown begins
- [ ] Collect and store all scoring records
- [ ] Break down stages and return equipment to storage
- [ ] Debrief volunteers; log any issues for future reference

## Range Officer Procedures

### Pre-Stage Brief (for each competitor)
Deliver this brief before the competitor approaches the line:

> "This is [Stage Name]. You have [N] targets. The stop plate is [location]. You will fire [N] strings. Do you have any questions about the course of fire? [Answer questions.] Load and make ready."

### Range Commands

| Command | Meaning |
|---------|---------|
| "Load and Make Ready" | Competitor may draw, load, and assume start position |
| "Are You Ready?" | Confirm competitor is ready; silence = ready |
| "Standby" | Start signal is imminent |
| *[Beep]* | Begin shooting |
| "Stop" | Cease fire immediately — do not shoot |
| "Unload and Show Clear" | Unload firearm, show empty chamber |
| "Hammer Down, Holster" | Drop hammer on empty chamber, holster |
| "Range is Safe" | Competitor and RO may handle targets, scoring complete |

### Calling a Stop
Call "STOP" immediately for:
- Any safety violation (muzzle direction, finger on trigger when not shooting)
- Equipment malfunction that could cause injury
- Range condition that poses a hazard
- Competitor appears injured or confused

---

## Scoring

- Scores are recorded immediately after each string
- All misses are recorded; no assumptions
- Procedural errors are noted and applied per current match rules
- Disputes are addressed by the match coordinator; RO decision is not final if coordinator is present
