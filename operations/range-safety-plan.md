## Range Safety Inspection Checklists

### Pre-Session Inspection (before every practice or match)

- [ ] Berms and backstops are intact and free of damage
- [ ] Firing line is clear and properly marked
- [ ] Target frames are stable and properly positioned
- [ ] Spectator area is marked and accessible
- [ ] First aid kit is stocked and accessible (location: TBD)
- [ ] Fire extinguisher is present and charged (location: TBD)
- [ ] Emergency exit routes are clear and unobstructed
- [ ] All known hazards are flagged or removed

### Post-Session Inspection (after every practice or match)

- [ ] All firearms confirmed unloaded
- [ ] Range policed for brass, debris, and equipment
- [ ] All targets and frames removed or secured
- [ ] No live ammunition left downrange
- [ ] Equipment stored properly
- [ ] Any damage or hazards documented and reported

## Incident Documentation

All safety incidents must be documented. Use the incident report in [operations/safety-manual.md](safety-manual.md).

Minimum documentation:
- Date, time, location
- Names of all involved
- Description of the incident
- Immediate actions taken
- Follow-up required
