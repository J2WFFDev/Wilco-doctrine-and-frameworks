# Match Director Guide

> **Canonical terminology reference:** [doctrine/terminology.md](../doctrine/terminology.md)
>
> **Authority note:** The Match Director holds final authority on all match-day decisions within the scope defined below. This document defines what is within that scope, what is delegated to the Assistant Match Director, and what is reserved to the Match Director alone.

---

## Role of the Match Director

The Match Director (MD) is the senior operational authority for any Wilco Shooting Sports match. The MD is accountable for:

- Safe, fair, and orderly match execution
- Final rules interpretations and scoring disputes
- Overall volunteer coordination
- Athlete and family experience
- Post-match documentation and reporting

## Match Director Authority: Reserved Decisions

The following decisions are **reserved to the Match Director** and may not be delegated:

- Final rulings on scoring protests and appeals
- Disqualification of a competitor
- Match cancellation or significant schedule modification
- Final authority on safety incidents that affect competition integrity
- Issuance of reshoot decisions (time-loss or equipment failure)

---

## Assistant Match Director

The Assistant Match Director (AMD) serves as a designated delegate and backup authority. The AMD:

- Holds full MD authority when the MD is temporarily unavailable
- Owns specific operational functions formally delegated by the MD
- Is the first point of contact for operational questions during the match, reducing demand on the MD

## Match Operating Committees

For larger matches, the MD may organize operations using functional committees. Each committee owns a specific phase of match operations.

### Range Prep Committee

**Purpose:** Set up and confirm all stages are safe and ready before the match opens.

**Lead:** Assistant Match Director (or designated volunteer)

**Responsibilities:**
> *Placeholder — stage layout confirmation, target inspection, berm and backstop check, prop setup, safe storage of tools before competition begins.*

**Completion criteria:** MD receives written/verbal confirmation from committee lead that all stages are safe and match-ready.

---

### Match Day Startup Committee

**Purpose:** Coordinate all opening activities from first volunteer arrival through the first shot.

**Lead:** AMD or designated coordinator

**Responsibilities:**
> *Placeholder — volunteer check-in, competitor registration and equipment check, safety brief, RSO assignments, scoring system initialization, timer/tablet distribution.*

**Completion criteria:** All competitors have checked in, safety brief completed, all RSOs are staged, scoring systems confirmed operational.

---

### Awards Committee

**Purpose:** Prepare and execute the awards ceremony.

**Lead:** Designated volunteer

**Responsibilities:**
> *Placeholder — score collection and validation, awards preparation, ceremony setup, result publication coordination with Match Score Technician.*

**Completion criteria:** Final scores published, awards presented, results communicated to program record system.

---

### Teardown Committee

**Purpose:** Safe and organized range teardown following competition close.

**Lead:** AMD or designated coordinator

**Responsibilities:**
> *Placeholder — confirm all firearms are unloaded and cased/holstered before teardown begins, stage deconstruction, target and equipment storage, range cleanup, final range safety check.*

**Completion criteria:** Range returned to pre-match condition, all equipment inventoried and stored, range cleared of all competitors and spectators.

## Rules Interpretation

> *Placeholder — define how rules interpretation works at Wilco matches. Which rulebook governs? (SCSA, NRA, etc.) What is the MD's process for making a ruling? Who may appeal to whom?*

**Ruling hierarchy:**
1. RSO makes initial safety/procedural call
2. Competitor may protest the call to the MD (not to the RSO)
3. MD makes a final ruling — the MD's ruling is final for that match
4. Post-match appeals (factual disputes, not judgment calls) may be raised with the Program Director

## Match Score Technician

The Match Score Technician (MST) is a specialized role responsible for the data infrastructure of the match. The MST is distinct from the Scorekeeper:

| Role | Primary Function |
|------|----------------|
| **Scorekeeper** | Field data recorder — records times and misses per string |
| **Match Score Technician** | Data systems — manages timers, tablets, sync, and publication |
