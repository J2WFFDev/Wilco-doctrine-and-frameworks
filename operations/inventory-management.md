## Purpose

This document defines how Wilco Shooting Sports tracks, inspects, stores, and manages program-owned equipment. Proper inventory management ensures:

- Equipment is available and functional when needed
- Safety equipment (hearing protection, eye protection, first aid) is always stocked
- Program-owned firearms and accessories are accounted for at all times
- Wear, damage, and loss are identified and addressed promptly
